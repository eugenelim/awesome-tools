# Wick

A Clock app for Mac with timers, a stopwatch and alarms that integrate with the menu bar and lock screen.[^wick]

## Features

From the vendor homepage:[^wick]

- Timers: multiple labelled timers, each with its own end sound and volume.
- Stopwatch with laps, split times and total times.
- Alarms with repeat, label, snooze duration and sound.
